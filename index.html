Canteen
Diaries

Six small activities about your lunch break.

We're a group of design students trying to understand what the college canteen is really like: the queue, the guessing, the tricks you use to survive. Not what it should be. What it's like for you.

Kit no. 1 · Group: Asawari · Yash · Miwa · Kimaya · Amruta · 2026–27

Before you begin

How this works

  1. Take your time. Do one page a day, or all at once. Your answers save on this device as you go.
  2. No right or wrong. Silly, honest and messy answers are the best ones.
  3. Photos and drawings upload right here in the kit. Voice notes are optional; if you'd like to send one, use WhatsApp.
  4. Skip anything you don't feel like doing.
Activity 1 of 6

The 15-minute break

Think of your last lunch break at the canteen. Where did the minutes go?

Slide each one until the bar is full at 15 minutes (or however long your break really was).

Activity 2 of 6

Queue view

Next time you're standing in the queue, take a photo of exactly what you see.

📸 Take a photo (on a phone this opens your camera) or pick one from your gallery, and upload it right here.

Then tell us how it felt.

Activity 3 of 6

The guess-o-meter

Before you walk to the canteen, how much do you actually know?

0 = total guess · 10 = I know exactly

Activity 4 of 6

Activity 5 of 6

Wishes & workarounds

A canteen genie gives you exactly two wishes for tomorrow's lunch rush.

What's the sneakiest trick you use to beat the rush?

Activity 6 of 6

Postcard to the canteen
